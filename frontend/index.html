How a report gets to the right desk

Four steps, no account needed. The photo and your location do most of the work.

1

Snap a photo

Use your camera or pick an existing photo of the problem.

2

We detect & locate

The issue type is guessed from the photo and your GPS position is read automatically.

3

You confirm it

Correct the category if needed, then submit. Nothing sends without this check.

4

Alert sent & tracked

An email goes out immediately, and the report appears on the dashboard for everyone to follow.

What it can tell apart

Four categories, each with its own flag so a full dashboard reads at a glance.

Pothole

Deep or shallow, spotted from a street-level photo.

Garbage

Overflowing bins and dumped waste piles.

Broken streetlight

Dark stretches of road, flagged from a night photo.

Water leakage

Burst pipes and standing leaks on the road or pavement.

The detector is a fast, explainable baseline, not a trained model. It's intentionally cautious, and flags low-confidence guesses for you to correct before anything is sent. See the project's README for how it works and how to swap in a trained model later.
